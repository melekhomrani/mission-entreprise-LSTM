DL Objective 1 — Job Market Demand Forecasting (LSTM)
Business Objective: Companies hiring data professionals and candidates planning their job search need to know where demand is heading, not just where it stands today. Forecasting posting volume per role type for the next 3 to 6 months allows recruiters to plan headcount and candidates to time their search strategically.
Data Source: External multi-year jobs dataset — Bureau of Labor Statistics monthly employment data by occupation, or a comparable multi-year job postings dataset from Kaggle. This dataset is independent from the main CSV and maintained as a separate pipeline.
Data Science Objective: Train an LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) neural network on monthly job posting volume per role category as a time series. The model learns temporal patterns including seasonality, growth trends, and cyclical variations that classical models handle poorly when patterns are nonlinear or involve multiple interacting series.
Why DL and not ML: An LSTM maintains a hidden state across time steps, learning long-range dependencies in the sequence — for example that a dip in Q1 typically precedes a spike in Q3. Classical ML treats each time step as independent features and cannot learn those sequential patterns.
Evaluation: RMSE and MAE on a held-out forecast window. Forecast output optionally surfaced as a forward-looking KPI in the BI dashboards.
